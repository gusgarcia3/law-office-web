02 / ABOUT

Gustavo A. Garcia

Gustavo Alejandro Garcia is a Florida attorney who advises clients on real estate, construction, and development matters in South Florida. His background in architecture and development informs his approach to legal issues involving property and construction.

He earned his J.D. from St. Thomas University School of Law and a Bachelor of Architecture from Florida International University. He has been a LEED Accredited Professional since 2009 and is an alumnus of ULI’s Leadership Institute, Class of 2022.
